PRIVACY BY ARCHITECTURE · RADICAL HONESTY

Your keys never touch Roadstr. Your trips never fund an ad network.

🔐

Zero Private Keys in the App

Roadstr intentionally removed pasted nsec login. Authenticate exclusively through Amber (NIP-55) or a remote Bunker (NIP-46) so your signing key stays isolated outside the app.

🛡

NIP-44 End-to-End Sync

Saved places and your parking spot stay local-only by default. If you enable cross-device sync or Journey shards over Nostr, payloads are encrypted with NIP-44 v2 to your own key — relays see only ciphertext.

📡

Zero Google Play Services

Reads GPS directly from Android's native LocationManager. Runs seamlessly on GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, LineageOS, and any degoogled Android device with zero analytics or telemetry SDKs.

🧭

Honest Threat Model

Roadstr runs no first-party servers. Online routing, Overpass lookups, Transitous, and weather send anonymous requests without persistent IDs. Use offline maps or a VPN if you want to hide IP-linked coordinates — and remember public Nostr/LoRa reports are pseudonymous, not anonymous.
