Visual redesign: a new home dashboard, refined glass-style panels
throughout, and a simplified theme lineup.

Changed

• New collapsible home dashboard on the map screen.
• Frosted-glass styling applied consistently across the bottom bar,
  navigation HUD, route panels and search — one shared design
  language instead of several ad-hoc looks.
• Simplified theming: dropped the "modern" gradient variants, keeping
  the four classic Nostr/Bitcoin themes (light and dark). Anyone who
  had picked a modern variant falls back to its closest classic
  counterpart automatically.
• Cleaned up the bottom bar: removed a small logo that was crowding
  the notifications/profile/menu buttons, giving them more room.
