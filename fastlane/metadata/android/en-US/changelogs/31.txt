A crash fixed at the root, clearer zone warnings, and a new look.

Fixed

• The app could die outright mid-drive. The cause was a memory bug inside
  the bundled speech library, which had not been updated since December
  2024 — twelve memory-safety fixes had landed upstream since, including
  ones in exactly the code that reads out distances and numbers. The
  library is rebuilt past those fixes
• The map no longer swings round abruptly after leaving a roundabout or a
  slip road, right when you are checking whether you took the right exit
• A destination chosen while the GPS is still acquiring is no longer
  thrown away — it is saved to your recent searches and the route starts
  on its own as soon as the position arrives
• Cold GPS fixes should be quicker, without Google: the app now asks the
  receiver to refresh its own satellite assistance data

Limited traffic zones

• Only the stretches of route actually on a restricted street turn red.
  The rest of the route keeps its colour, and the part already driven
  stays grey
• "You are in a restricted zone" now appears only when you really are in
  one, based on your live position — not because the route passes nearby
• Driving past a restricted street shows a yellow notice naming it, so a
  tempting shortcut is not mistaken for an open road
• Restricted streets near the route are drawn dotted in red

Look and feel

• Four new themes: modern light and dark, in Nostr Violet and Bitcoin
  Orange
• The route is drawn as a lit line with a bright core and a soft halo
• Driving panels now float above the map with rounded corners and lit
  edges, and the manoeuvre tile and map controls are lit surfaces
• Time to arrival is now the headline figure, with distance beside it
• Bicycle routes can be planned from the start, not only chosen after a
  route has been calculated
• The exit number on the roundabout symbol is larger and no longer
  clipped on roundabouts with many exits
