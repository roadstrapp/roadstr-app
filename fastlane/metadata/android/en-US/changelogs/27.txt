A crash fixed, and the roundabout signs corrected.

• Fixed a crash during navigation. The map opened a new database watcher on
  every GPS fix and never closed the old ones fast enough; after a few
  minutes of driving the app ran out of room and died
• Roundabout signs showed too many arms. Where a road meets a roundabout as
  two separate carriageways it was counted twice — a four-exit junction drew
  as seven. When the shape still cannot be worked out reliably, a plain
  roundabout is drawn instead of a wrong one
• Distances over a kilometre are spoken as kilometres. "In 4567 metres" is
  now "in 4.5 kilometres", with the decimal read correctly in your language
• One instruction no longer cuts off another mid-sentence. Advance warnings
  wait their turn; only the final "now" instruction takes over
• The map no longer occasionally spins 180°. Standing still the GPS drifts,
  and the direction was being measured from where it had drifted to
• More restricted-traffic zones are recognised. Most are tagged in
  OpenStreetMap as time-limited, and those tags were not being read at all
• The secondary instruction panel is narrower, so it covers less of the map
