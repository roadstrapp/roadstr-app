Fixes from real driving: a crash, a swinging map, and voice timing.

Fixed

• GPS no longer drops when you switch away from the app outside
  navigation. It used to release the location receiver the instant the
  app left the foreground, even for a three-second glance at a
  notification, forcing a fresh fix on every return. It now waits 30
  seconds of actually being backgrounded first
• A crash while panning the map during navigation exposed a missing
  safety net: no unhandled exception outside Flutter's own build cycle
  was being caught anywhere, so any one of them could take the whole app
  down. They are now caught and logged instead
• The map could still swing on sharp turns after an earlier roundabout
  fix. A second cause was hiding behind it: once a large heading
  correction was confirmed, the camera snapped straight to the route's
  exact direction instead of easing into it. It now eases the same way
  ordinary corrections do
• The final spoken warning before a turn was capped at 120 metres of
  lead distance no matter the speed — 3.6 seconds at 120 km/h, enough to
  hear the instruction but not enough to act on it. It now keeps scaling
  with speed, up to 260 metres at 160 km/h
• In a run of close-together junctions, spoken instructions could queue
  up and describe turns already behind you. A stale warning is now
  dropped instead of queued when a fresher one is on the way
• The hazard-report button used to sit on the left before navigation and
  jump to the right once it started — the only control that moved sides.
  It now stays on the right in both states
