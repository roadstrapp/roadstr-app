Stops along the way.

• A journey can now visit up to four places on the way to its destination.
  Add them in the route planner, one search field per stop
• Drag the rows by the grip to change the order — and the order matters:
  the same places visited in a different sequence is a different journey,
  and the route redraws to match
• The destination keeps its own marker wherever you drag it to, so the
  end of the journey stays recognisable
• Already driving? The new button on the right adds a stop without
  stopping. It uses the same search as the home screen — address,
  places, favourites, nearby — and the route recalculates from where you
  are now, then guidance simply carries on
• The voice control moved under the instruction panel to make room

Stops left empty are ignored rather than refused, and alternatives are
not offered once a route is pinned through a stop — the router declines to
compute them, so asking only costs a wait.
