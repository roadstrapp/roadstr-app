Pick your own colours, and a new way to walk.

• Seven colour tints for the vehicle cursor — violet, indigo, blue, green,
  yellow, orange, red — chosen from Settings alongside the existing style
  picker

• Walking navigation now shows the ostrich actually walking or running,
  smoothly switching pace with your speed, instead of a static icon

  could start it playing before all its frames were loaded, causing a
