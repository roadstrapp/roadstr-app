Roundabout signs that match the junction you are actually approaching.

• The roundabout symbol now draws the real number of arms, read from
  OpenStreetMap, instead of inferring them from the exit number. "Take the
  third exit" looks different at a four-arm junction than at a six-arm one,
  and now the sign does too
• New cursor styles to choose from — city, racing, SUV, electric, bicycle,
  formula — alongside the existing arrow
• The speedometer has been redrawn
• The extra map lookups this needs are capped, so a route through fifty
  roundabouts cannot turn into one enormous request to a volunteer-run
  OpenStreetMap server, and no more of your itinerary leaves the device than
  the signs actually need
