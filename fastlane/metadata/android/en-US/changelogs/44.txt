Crosswalk/speed-bump map icons, better search ranking, and a couple of
route-planner fixes.

Added

• Crosswalks and speed bumps now show on the map at their exact
  OSM-mapped position, sourced the same way the speed-camera and
  traffic-light markers already are.

Fixed

• Search results now list confident matches nearest-first — a
  franchise's own branches no longer get shuffled by minor wording
  differences outranking real distance.
• Typing a city name at the end of a search ("mercatino usato
  faenza") now prioritises results actually in that city over merely
  closer ones elsewhere.
• The two-point route planner ("Da" field) now works even without a
  GPS fix yet, falling back to the current map view.
• Long-press on the map: "report event here" is back at the exact
  long-pressed point, alongside "set parking here".
• A "location services are off" prompt now appears when tapping the
  recentre button with no GPS fix, instead of doing nothing.
