Public transport, and a calmer map.

• Plan a journey by bus, tram, metro, train or ferry. Pick "On foot" in the
  route planner and a sub-choice appears underneath
• Journeys show every stage door to door: the walk to the stop, which stop
  to head for and by when, the line to catch in its operator's own colour,
  the changes, and the walk at the other end
• Works worldwide from openly published timetables, with no account and no
  API key. Areas without published data say so plainly instead of
  pretending there is no service
• Bicycle routes can now be planned from the start, not only chosen after
  a route has already been calculated

Fixed

• The map no longer swings round abruptly after leaving a roundabout or a
  slip road, right when you are checking whether you took the correct exit
• A destination chosen while the GPS is still acquiring is no longer
  thrown away — it is remembered, saved to your recent searches, and the
  route starts on its own as soon as the position arrives
• Cold GPS fixes should be quicker: the app now asks the receiver to
  refresh its satellite assistance data, which is what otherwise forces it
  to spend half a minute reading that data off the satellites themselves
• The exit number on the roundabout symbol is larger, and no longer
  clipped on roundabouts with many exits
• Saving or syncing favourites no longer freezes the app. The password
  step is deliberately slow by design, and it was running on the same
  thread as the interface — on every favourite edit and at every startup
• Requests that fail because a shared server is momentarily busy now back
  off and retry, respecting the server's own requested delay, instead of
  being treated the same as a permanently broken request
