Hotfix: 0.5.1's battery optimization broke the MapLibre screen entirely.

Fixed

• 0.5.1 disabled a rendering mode to save battery/heat. On real devices
  this instead hid every on-screen control (search bar, all buttons)
  behind the map itself, since the map's Android renderer stopped
  composing through Flutter the way the rest of the screen needed.
  Reverted — the battery/heat fixes from 0.5.1 that don't cause this are
  unaffected
