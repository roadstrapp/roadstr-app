Four field reports, four fixes.

• Search history is no longer hidden. Anyone with a saved favourite never
  saw their recent searches at all — the two lists were competing for the
  same slot, and favourites always won
• The map no longer flips 180° near junctions and roundabout exits. A
  single stray direction reading close to a roundabout could be trusted
  instantly; it now has to repeat before the map acts on it
• Voice guidance could go completely silent for an entire drive, then work
  again on the next one. A one-time setup step at the start of navigation
  had no retry — it now retries later in the same drive instead of making
  you start a new one
• Recalculating after a wrong turn is smarter about it. On a long road with
  nowhere to legally turn around, it used to keep proposing a route that
  assumed an instant reversal — over and over, with the map spinning each
  time. It now takes into account which way you're actually facing, so it
  finds a real way back (the next roundabout, the next junction) instead —
  with a safeguard against an absurd detour in either direction
