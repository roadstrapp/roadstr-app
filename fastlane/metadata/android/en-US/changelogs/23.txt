Voice guidance that says what it is about to do, and when.

• Instructions that appeared on screen but were never spoken are now spoken.
  Chaining only happened when the next turn was within 55 m, and being
  chained also cancelled that turn's own warnings — so a manoeuvre could be
  named once inside another sentence and never mentioned again
• When an instruction is repeated at the point of action, the next one is
  announced with it and with its distance: "take the first exit, then in
  300 metres take the off-ramp"
• The destination is only named within 3 km. Further out you are told how
  far the road runs instead, so "you will arrive" no longer arrives with
  ten miles still to drive
• Announcements now say the distance that is actually left. Accelerating
  onto a slip road used to widen the warning window mid-manoeuvre and call
  a ramp 380 m ahead "in 800 metres"
• Motorway exits are read from each router's real manoeuvre table. A right
  exit was being classified as a left turn, which is why its symbol looked
  like a merge going the wrong way
• Roads that change name mid-stretch no longer produce a burst of
  instructions before a junction — OpenStreetMap names consecutive sections
  of one road differently, and each rename was announced as a manoeuvre
• A roundabout with an unknown exit count no longer claims "first exit"
• Speed limits, speed cameras and restricted-zone lookups no longer keep a
  route from loading when a router reports an unusual exit number
