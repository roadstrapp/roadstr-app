A privacy audit of our own work, and what it turned up.

• Voice clips no longer outlive the journey. Spoken instructions are
  synthesised to audio files, and those files said your street names out
  loud — kept on the device indefinitely, with no way to clear them. Only
  fixed phrases are stored now; anything naming a place is deleted when
  navigation ends, at startup, and after a crash
• Exporting favourites is encrypted by default. The file holds home and
  work and leaves the app for your Downloads folder; protecting it is no
  longer something you have to remember to switch on
• Reported positions are published with 11 cm precision instead of 1 cm.
  GPS is accurate to metres, so the extra digit was noise attached to your
  identity
• The map no longer keeps its camera loop running while you sit still with
  heading-up mode on — compass noise was waking it sixty times a second
