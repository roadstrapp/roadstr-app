The cursor now points where you are actually going, and relays are
treated as hostile by default.

• Outside navigation the cursor and the map now follow your direction of
  travel while you are moving, instead of the phone's compass — no more
  arrow sideways to the road when the phone sits at an angle
• The compass takes over only when you are stopped, and the handover has
  a dead band so crawling in traffic no longer makes the map hesitate
• The cursor now turns with the map itself, so it stays lined up with the
  road all the way through a curve and after rotating the map by hand
• Starting navigation while already driving no longer swings the map to
  the compass first
• A GPS fix arriving mid-transition can no longer cut the route overview
  short when navigation starts
• Passphrase-protected favourites now restore automatically again after a
  reinstall, instead of being skipped
• You can now add a relay of your own to the favourites sync, in Settings.
  Public relays make no promise about how long they keep anything; one you
  run or pay for does

• Favourites sync now uses three relays instead of two, so deleting or
  restoring saved places no longer depends on a single relay being up
• Deleting your last favourite is now published like any other change,
  instead of leaving the old list on the relays
• Favourite uploads are queued, so two quick edits can no longer land in
  the wrong order and bring back a place you just deleted
• A relay can no longer grow the app's memory by streaming updates that
  point at reports which never arrive
• "My reports" now re-checks locally that each report is actually yours,
  and its own budget can no longer be spent by confirmations
• Sync no longer reports success when a relay acknowledges the wrong event
• Bounded work per history fetch, so a relay that ignores the requested
  limit cannot make the app verify signatures indefinitely
• Stricter address checks on Lightning address lookups, and a total
  deadline on the response instead of a per-chunk one
• Place names from search providers are length-capped before display
• Reconnects and map-data retries now back off instead of repeating at a
  fixed rate, which saves battery on a bad connection and stops the app
  from adding load to a service that is already struggling
• Search sends far less traffic while you type: suggestions now come from
  the provider built for partial words, and the strict address lookup runs
  once you stop typing rather than on every pause
