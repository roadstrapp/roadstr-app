Directions in your own language, and warnings you can trust.

• Turn-by-turn instructions now exist in all 27 languages. Until now only
  Italian and English had their own; everyone else was navigated in
  English
• Instructions name the road, and prefer the number on the sign — "take
  the SS3bis", not its formal name, which appears nowhere on the road

Limited traffic zones

• Warnings no longer appear on streets that are not restricted. The
  search was picking up every pedestrian way with no check on whether
  cars were actually barred, and in a historic centre there is almost
  always one within a few metres of a perfectly drivable street
• A warning could get stuck over the cursor until the app was restarted.
  Fixed
• Warnings sit clear of the cursor now, and can be swiped away — useful
  if you hold a permit for a zone you drive every day

Fixed

• The map no longer swings round now and then. The direction of the route
  was read from the nearest piece of it, and at a roundabout the exit arm
  passes within metres of the entry arm pointing the opposite way. It is
  now read from how far along the route you actually are
• Spoken instructions no longer break mid-sentence, and a cue arriving
  between two others is no longer silently skipped. Nothing is cut off in
  its first second and a bit
• "Bivio" and similar words are pronounced properly

Also

• The street you are on is shown under the manoeuvre panel, for town
  streets only — a motorway number is already on every sign
• The manoeuvre panel sits higher, the voice control moved beside the
  next-turn preview, and neither covers the speed limit
