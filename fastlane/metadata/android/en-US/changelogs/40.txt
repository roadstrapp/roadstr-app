MapLibre is now the default map engine, plus real-world fixes from driving with it.

Changed

• The MapLibre 3D rendering engine (introduced last release as an option)
  is now the default. The original renderer is still one tap away in
  Settings for anyone who prefers it

Fixed

• The cursor used to freeze between GPS fixes and jump to the next one —
  very visible at speed. It now advances smoothly between fixes instead
  of sitting still
• Fixed battery drain and device heat introduced by the smoothing above:
  a screen-wide rebuild was firing up to 60 times a second while driving,
  and MapLibre was rendering through a slower compatibility mode it
  didn't need
• Fixed the male voice not sticking after picking it in Settings, and
  made it the default
• Fixed route calculation feeling slow — a cosmetic detail (roundabout
  exit counts for voice instructions) was blocking the whole route
  picker from showing
• Added a long-press "set as parking" prompt on the map
