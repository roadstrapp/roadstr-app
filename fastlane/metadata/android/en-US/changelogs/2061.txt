Public transport is back in the Kotlin planner.

Added

• Real multimodal itineraries from Transitous/MOTIS v6, including walking, bus, tram, metro, rail and ferry legs when data is available
• Provider-supplied alternatives with line direction, operators, platforms, intermediate stops and scheduled/realtime times
• Delay, cancellation, accessibility and service-alert details without presenting scheduled data as realtime
• Expanded itinerary cards and MapLibre boarding/alighting markers

Improved

• Added a capability-aware transit provider boundary with bounded retries and provider-scoped identifiers
• Added visible provider attribution and updated the legacy Flutter request to the current stable MOTIS contract

Note: active transit guidance, saved transit journeys, stop/departure search and offline transit routing are planned follow-ups.
