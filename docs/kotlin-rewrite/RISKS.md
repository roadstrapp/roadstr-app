| R-25 | The Kotlin app stops guiding when the screen is off or the app is in the background | Critical | High | Navigation progress, voice cues and reroute run in Compose effects of the shell, and the activity stops GPS and voice in `onStop`; the Flutter app keeps the GPS alive with a foreground service. Navigation state must live outside the Activity (UI_PARITY), driven by a lifecycle-independent owner plus the foreground service and its notification | Closed on the device 2026-10-06, with the limits listed at the end. **Evidence before the fix, 2026-10-06:** on the Pixel 10, `dumpsys location` shows an active HIGH_ACCURACY GPS request of `app.roadstr` with the app in front and none four seconds after the Home key, and no foreground service of the app. **Device evidence after the fix, 2026-10-06** (candidate 0.6.0-rc3, a 2.1 km route in Verona driven by the shell's location test provider, removed afterwards): with the app sent to the background and then the screen off (the phone dozing, locked), the app kept both of its HIGH_ACCURACY GPS requests, its foreground service (location type) kept running, the notification followed the manoeuvres (511 m, then 227 m, then 'Ora') and carries a content intent, and navigation-guidance speech was playing from an AudioTrack with USAGE_ASSISTANCE_NAVIGATION_GUIDANCE while the phone was dozing; at the destination the notification was cleared at once, the service stopped after about 12 s and the GPS requests were released. Also checked on the device, same route: tapping the navigation notification brings the app back with the manoeuvre card, the next-step card, the speed-limit sign, the remaining time and distance consistent with the notification; a rotation in the middle of the trip kept the trip; with the app swept out of the recent-apps list the process (same pid), the foreground service, both GPS requests and the notification stayed, and the notification reopened the app on the same trip (the Activity is created again, the trip is the host's, not the Activity's); the exit button asks for confirmation, the dialog survives Home and a return, and confirming it removes the service and both notifications at once and releases the GPS as soon as the app is no longer in front (the process drops to a cached state). Limits: the fixes came from a test provider, so the speed read 0 km/h and the arrival phrase was seen only as an audio track, not heard; the Activity's destruction was not observed directly (the 'do not keep activities' developer option left the Activity stopped, not destroyed, and `dumpsys` kept a stale record after the sweep), so the case is covered by the sweep and by the host's own tests; a trip is held in memory only, so if the system kills the process (low memory, force stop) the trip, its service and its notification end and are not restored. |

Not a migration problem (these come from relays, not from stored data). |

## Current stop condition

No destructive framework-removal work may begin until R-01 through R-04 have
evidence and the migration prototype can preserve identity, protected storage,
favourites and model assets in fixtures.
